Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-04 · version 1.1

ProMeasure is provided by Parvinder Singh. It works entirely on your phone. We don’t have accounts, servers, ads or analytics of our own, and we don’t receive or sell any personal data. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google’s AR service sends to Google, described below.

What stays on your phone

Saved measurements, notes and photos, and your settings (units, calibration), are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We never see them. They are deleted if you delete them in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Photos are only saved when you tap save in AR measuring. They show the camera view with the measurements drawn on top.

Camera and sensors

The camera is used live to measure, show the camera level and aim for height by angle. Camera images are processed on the phone and not recorded unless you save a measurement.

The accelerometer, magnetometer, barometer and light sensor are read only while you use a tool that needs them, and the readings are not stored except for values you choose to save.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). ARCore processes camera images on your phone to track motion and find surfaces; camera images are not sent to Google. To run and improve the service, ARCore sends Google a device or Google account identifier, performance and diagnostic data, and records of how the app uses ARCore. Google uses this for analytics and to keep ARCore working, does not share it with third parties, and sends it encrypted. We never receive it. Learn more: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.ar.core

Sharing

When you share a measurement, photo or CSV export, it goes only where you send it, using your phone’s share menu. That app’s own privacy policy then applies.

Google Play

Google Play may collect information about installs and crashes under Google’s own privacy policy. We see only the anonymous, aggregated statistics Google provides to developers.

Children

ProMeasure is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We collect no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app. Questions or requests: ps.promeasureapp@gmail.com.
