Measurements are estimates

Every value ProMeasure shows comes from your phone’s camera and sensors, which are not calibrated measuring instruments. Results depend on the phone, lighting, the surface, how you move and how far away you are. Typical AR accuracy is about ±1–3 cm at 1–2 m and gets worse with distance; the level is typically within ±0.1–0.5° once calibrated; floor height, light and metal readings are rougher still.

Don’t rely on ProMeasure alone for anything where an error could cause injury, damage, loss or a legal or financial consequence. That includes construction, structural, electrical or plumbing work, ordering or cutting materials, property or legal measurements, medical or safety purposes, and anything to do with machinery or vehicles. Confirm with a certified tape, laser measure, level or a qualified professional.

Metal finder

The metal finder senses changes in the magnetic field. It only reacts to iron and steel. It does not detect wood, copper or plastic pipes, aluminium, or live electrical wires, and it can miss metal entirely. Never use it to decide that a spot is safe to drill, cut or nail. Use a proper detector and turn off power and water where needed.

Use it safely

Watch where you are going while using the camera. Don’t use ProMeasure while driving, on ladders or roofs, near edges, roads, water or moving machinery, or anywhere looking at a screen could put you or others at risk. Supervise children using it.

Features depend on your phone

Some tools need hardware that not every phone has: AR measuring needs a phone supported by Google Play Services for AR; floor height needs a barometer; the metal finder needs a magnetometer; the light meter needs a light sensor the phone shares with apps. ProMeasure shows which tools your phone supports. A missing sensor is not a fault in the app.
